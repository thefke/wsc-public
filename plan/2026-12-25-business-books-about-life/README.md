# Fr 25.12. · Business books that are secretly about life

Abend-Slot. Karussell, 9 Slides. Beim Hochladen 4:5 wählen.

## Caption

```
“I don't read business books.”
Then read the ones that are secretly about life.

Restaurants. Investing. Hiring. Strategy.
On the surface it's work.
Underneath it's how to live.

Danny Meyer in Setting the Table:
“Business, like life, is all about how you make people feel. It's that simple, and it's that hard.”

All from my shelf.

Send this to the friend who skips the business shelf.

Which one would you start with?

```
