# Do 15.10. · What to read on a 10-hour flight

Karussell, 8 Slides. Beim Hochladen 4:5 wählen.

## Caption

```
Ten hours in a seat.
Bad screen. Worse Wi-Fi.

Six true stories from my shelf
that are hard to put down.

One detail from Endurance:
The ship froze into the ice
a day's sail short of land.

Which one goes in your carry-on?
```
